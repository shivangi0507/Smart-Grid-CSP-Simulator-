The CSP the agent solves every tick
Variables (what we decide): K blackout level, G gas level, P price, B battery command.
Domains (what we can choose): K∈{0..4} (×4% of load) · G∈{0..6} (gas steps) · P∈{$0.10 … $0.40, 9 steps} · B∈{−4..+4} (− discharge, + charge).
Constraints (which choices are allowed):
  • Unary: P ≤ regulatory cap ($0.40, or $0.26 in extreme heat/cold) · battery energy within 10–100% · no charge↔discharge flip within 3 steps · K=0 in extreme weather (human safety)
  • Binary: no charging battery with gas (G,B) · blackout only after gas is maxed (K,G) · blackout only after price is at cap (K,P)
  • Higher-order over (K,G,P,B): frequency band (deficit ≤ 4%) · no wasted gas, battery or blackout
Solver: backtracking, smallest-domain-first variable order, forward checking with domain wipe-out (DWO) detection. All solutions are collected, then the one with the best utility is chosen: renewable used − gas − blackout − battery wear − price surge.
